Groups and departments

Organise {{ org.name }} your way. Departments are offices or units (e.g. Finance, Sales), and can sit inside each other. Groups are sets of people (e.g. Form 2, Youth, Corporate clients). Both appear in the Group list when you add a contact, and you can place members in them. Roles and job titles are on the Roles and titles page.
